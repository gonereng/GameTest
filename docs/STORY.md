# The Moon Is in My Turnip

*A one-hour story quest for Hollowbrook.*

> One night the moon falls out of the sky and lands in your turnip patch.
> It talks. Its name is Gerald. It would like to go home now.

---

## The premise

The moon hangs over Hollowbrook on a glowing **Moonstring**. Slimes are made of leftover moonlight,
and lately they've been fading. They decided that if the moon were *closer*, they'd feel better,
so they quietly chewed through the string.

It worked a bit too well. The moon fell into your farm.

**The deadline:** if Gerald isn't back in the sky by **midnight on Day 4**, the night never ends.
(The clock literally stops at 11:59 PM, the screen stays dark, and everyone in town starts
speaking only in whispers.)

**The twist:** Mayor Orla is three slimes stacked in a trench coat. They're named **O**llie,
**R**udy and **La**rs. They've run the town for six years, and honestly they've done a great job.

---

## Cast

| Who | What they are |
| --- | --- |
| **Gerald** | The Moon. Currently a glowing turnip about the size of a cow, half buried in a crater on your farm. Dramatic, polite, terrified of being eaten. |
| **Mayor Orla** | Secretly Ollie, Rudy and Lars. Drips a little. Can't stand near the fountain (it "tickles"). |
| **Pip** | The only person in town who noticed. Nobody believes Pip, because Pip once claimed a mushroom was "THIS big". (It was.) |
| **Bram** | Sells anything, asks no questions. In the epilogue it turns out Bram is the Sun, working a side job. |
| **Lord Gloopington III** | All the stolen moonlight, curdled into one enormous, very sad slime. The final boss. |
| **Slime Librarian** | Guards the Slime Burrow archive. Wears tiny glasses. Has no eyes. |

---

## Timeline at a glance

A full in-game day is about 14 real minutes, so the quest spans roughly four days.

| Act | In-game time | Real time | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Normal life | Day 1 | ~10 min | The normal tutorial: farm, meet the town. At 10 PM a loud **WHUMP** shakes the screen. The moon vanishes from the sky. |
| 1. The Turnip | Day 2 morning | ~10 min | Meet Gerald. Ask around town. Orla acts weird; Pip has a lead. |
| 2. The Slime Burrow | Day 2 afternoon to Day 3 | ~20 min | Find the secret burrow, put on a slime disguise, answer riddles, recover the Moonstring, learn the truth. Unmask Orla. |
| 3. The Turnipult | Day 3 to Day 4 evening | ~15 min | Grow crops at hyper-speed in Gerald's glow, buy parts, build a catapult. |
| 4. Midnight | Day 4, 10 PM to midnight | ~5 min | Lord Gloopington attacks the farm. Win (or sing it to sleep), then launch Gerald. |

## Act 0: Something falls (Day 1)

Ordinary day. The player learns farming and meets Orla and Pip as usual. Small hints:

- Orla's dialogue: *"Lovely moon tonight. Big. Round. Not that I'd know anything about it."*
- Looking at the fountain near Orla makes her say *"Please stop looking at the fountain."*

**10:00 PM:** wherever you are, the camera shakes, a white flash fills the screen, and a toast reads
**"Something just fell on your farm."** The night overlay becomes much darker for the rest of the
game, until Gerald goes back up.

If the player is already asleep, they wake on Day 2 to a crater outside anyway.

## Act 1: There is a moon in my turnip (Day 2 morning)

A 2x2 crater has replaced part of the field. In it sits a **giant glowing turnip**. Press E:

> **Gerald:** Ah. Hello. Please don't eat me.
> **Gerald:** I am the Moon. You may call me Gerald.
> **Gerald:** I seem to have fallen into your vegetable. It was the nearest soft thing.
> **Gerald:** Someone cut my string. If I am not back up by midnight in three days,
> the night will simply... continue. Forever. Which I'm told is bad for crops.

**Quest step:** *Ask around town about the Moonstring.*

**In town:**
- **Orla:** *"Moon? Never heard of her. Have you tried NOT looking for it?"* Wet footprint tiles
  appear wherever Orla has stood.
- **Pip:** *"I SAW slimes carrying a glowing rope into the woods! Past the bridge, where the
  bushes make a circle! Nobody believes me!"* Pip asks for **3 Slime Gel** "for science". Giving
  it unlocks Pip's **Kazoo** later (see Act 4).
- **Bram:** *"Moon fell? Happens. I've got a ladder. It's not long enough. Nothing is."*

## Act 2: The Slime Burrow (Day 2 afternoon to Day 3)

### The bush ring
East of the bridge in Mossy Woods there's a ring of 8 bushes. One of them **wobbles**. Hitting
it with the sword reveals a slime in a bush costume, which shrieks and runs. Behind it is a hole:
**the entrance to the Slime Burrow** (new map).

### "Only slimes may pass"
Inside, a door made of jelly says *ONLY SLIMES MAY PASS*. Slimes in the burrow are not
aggressive. They're on a break.

**Puzzle:** become a slime.
- Bram sells a **Slime Suit** for **5 Slime Gel + 50g**. (*"I don't ask questions."*)
- Selecting the suit in the hotbar puts it on: the player sprite turns into a green wobbling
  blob with the player's hair on top. Slimes stop attacking while it's worn.
- Every slime in the burrow can now be talked to. Highlights:
  - *"We're unionized. Six squish breaks a day."*
  - *"The Big Round One used to hum us to sleep. Now we only hum ourselves."*
  - *"Don't tell anyone, but Mayor Orla is—" (another slime shushes it)*

### The Slime Librarian's riddles
The archive door is guarded by the **Slime Librarian**, who asks three questions. Every answer
appeared earlier in the game, so attentive players are rewarded. Wrong answers just knock you
back to the start of the questions (and the Librarian sighs).

1. *"What grows only on nights after it has been cried on?"* → **A watered crop**
   (Orla says this on Day 1).
2. *"How many people live in Hollowbrook?"* → **23 and a few slimes** (the town sign).
3. *"How big was the mushroom?"* → **THIS big** (Pip). The other choices are "Normal size",
   "Small" and "There was no mushroom".

### The truth
In the archive, on a pedestal, is the **Moonstring**, glowing and neatly coiled. Taking it
plays a short scene:

> **Slime Librarian:** We didn't mean for it to *fall*.
> **Slime Librarian:** We're made of moonlight. Every year there's less of us.
> We only wanted it a little closer. Like sitting near a fire.
> **Slime Librarian:** Our leader said it was a good plan. Our leader is very busy. Our leader
> is the mayor.

**Quest step:** *Confront Mayor Orla.*

### The unmasking (Day 3)
Show Orla the Moonstring. The trench coat collapses and three slimes tumble out:
**Ollie, Rudy and Lars.**

> **Ollie:** Okay. Okay okay okay.
> **Rudy:** In our defense, the town budget has been balanced for six years.
> **Lars:** We fixed the fountain. We invented Tuesday market. We are *good at mayor*.

The player chooses:
- **"You can stay mayor. Help me fix this."** The three join you for the finale as helpers.
- **"Get out of my town."** They flee to the woods. The finale is harder, and the epilogue is a
  bit sadder.

## Act 3: The Turnipult (Day 3 to Day 4 evening)

The string alone isn't enough. Gerald needs a running start.

> **Gerald:** What I need is to be *thrown*. Very hard. At midnight, when the sky is softest.

**Build the Turnipult** next to the crater. It needs three parts:

| Part | How to get it |
| --- | --- |
| **Elastic** | 10 Slime Gel. If Ollie, Rudy and Lars are helping, they donate 5. |
| **Frame** | Bram's **Suspiciously Large Spoon**, 400g. |
| **Counterweight** | One **Moonmelon**. Normally 8 days to grow... |

**The trick:** crops planted **right next to Gerald's crater** bathe in moonlight. If watered,
they grow **one stage per in-game hour** instead of once per night. A Moonmelon planted on the
morning of Day 3 is ripe by the evening. Carrots grown here sell like normal, so this is also how
to pay for the 400g spoon.

Moon-grown crops have a faint glow. Pip says *"those vegetables are looking at me."*

Interacting with the crater once all three parts are in the bag builds the Turnipult (a big
wooden spoon on a jelly spring with a giant turnip in it).

**Quest step:** *Wait for midnight on Day 4.*

## Act 4: Midnight (Day 4, 10 PM to midnight)

### Lord Gloopington III
At **10 PM** the ground rumbles. All the moonlight the slimes hoarded has curdled into one
enormous slime, **Lord Gloopington III**, which oozes out of the forest onto your farm, heading
straight for the Turnipult.

> **Lord Gloopington:** THE BIG ROUND ONE STAYS. THE BIG ROUND ONE IS *OURS*.

**Boss fight (on the farm):**
1. **Phase 1:** he lunges in straight lines. Dodge sideways and hit him from behind.
2. **Phase 2 (half health):** he splits into four medium slimes, which merge back after 15 seconds.
   If you pardoned Ollie, Rudy and Lars, they each grab one and hold it in place.
3. **Phase 3:** he starts to cry. Real tears that water the soil, and every crop on the farm grows
   one stage in front of you. He stops attacking.

**Two endings:**
- **Fight:** keep hitting him. He bursts into a shower of glowing gel that rains up into the sky.
- **Lullaby:** if you gave Pip the gel in Act 1, Pip runs in with the **Kazoo**. Choose
  *"Play the lullaby"* and the player kazoos the tune Gerald used to hum. Gloopington falls
  asleep, shrinks, and turns into a small, happy slime that follows you around for the rest of the
  game (a pet!).

### Launch
At **midnight**, interact with the Turnipult:

> **Gerald:** Thank you, farmer. Do tell the slimes I'll hum louder.
> **Gerald:** And if anyone asks, I was never in a turnip.

The screen shakes, Gerald flies up off the top of the screen with the Moonstring trailing behind,
and the night overlay snaps back to normal. A big pale moon appears in the night sky from now on.
**There is a carrot stuck in it.** It's the counterweight. It's there forever.

## Epilogue (Day 5 morning)

- The crater is now a permanent **Moonpatch**: a 2x2 spot where crops grow twice as fast.
- If pardoned, Ollie, Rudy and Lars are back in the trench coat at town hall. Everyone knows.
  Nobody minds. Pip is unbearable about being right.
- **Bram**, unprompted, while you shop:
  > *"Nice work with the moon. Professional courtesy."*
  > *"Same time next eclipse?"*
  > (Bram's eyes glow like two tiny suns. Then the shop just continues being a shop.)
- Toast: **"Quest complete: The Moon Is in My Turnip."**

## Failure

If Day 5 begins with Gerald still in the crater, the clock freezes at 11:59 PM:

> *The night goes on. And on. Somewhere, a slime hums alone.*

Options: **Try again from the morning of Day 4** (an extra save is made at the start of Day 4),
or **keep playing in eternal night**. Crops still grow, but every NPC whispers.

## Implementation notes

What the current code needs, roughly in build order:

- **Quest state:** add `quest: { step, flags }` to `GameState` (saved with everything else), plus
  a small quest log in the Esc menu showing the current goal.
- **Scripted events:** time triggers in `WorldScene.tickClock` (the 10 PM crash on Day 1, the boss
  on Day 4, freezing the clock at 11:59 PM), plus one-off scenes on map entry.
- **New map:** `burrow` (slime-themed tiles: jelly floor, jelly door, pedestal), entered through the
  bush ring in the forest.
- **New NPCs:** Gerald (2x2 sprite in a crater on the farm), Slime Librarian, Ollie, Rudy, Lars,
  and friendly slime NPCs in the burrow.
- **New items:** Moonstring, Slime Suit, Kazoo, Suspiciously Large Spoon. The Turnipult is a
  placed object.
- **Mechanics:** the Slime Suit (player sprite swap, slimes stay passive), the moon-glow growth
  boost next to the crater, the boss with three phases, and helpers.
- **Dialogue:** the dialogue box already supports choices; the riddles and the pardon decision can
  use it directly.
- **Visuals:** a darker night while the moon is missing, a moon with a carrot in it afterwards,
  wet footprint tiles behind Orla, and glowing moon-grown crops.
